01

Astrology readings — a D2C product, end to end

Online astrology readings by an astrologer · ads, funnel, offer, tracking+

The product

Kundli-matching and relationship readings by an astrologer, sold online to people in India looking for an answer about a marriage or relationship.

Ads and creative

Meta Sales campaigns with audience and creative tests; budget moved to the ad that produced paid orders.

Landing page and funnel

An animated landing page with an A/B variant and a free first answer before payment, then a Razorpay checkout.

Offer

A four-tier offer ladder, from a small order bump to a premium reading.

Tracking

Every order reconciled against Razorpay. That check found the Meta Pixel double-counting purchases, and test orders marked as paid — so budget decisions use captured payments only.

02

Room heater — one product, three versions of the campaign

D2C product · creative iteration+

The setup

Same room heater, three Sales campaigns. When a version wasn't working I rebuilt it instead of putting more money into it.

What happened

Click-through rate went from 1.30% on the first version to 2.22% and 2.17% on the next two. Version two got 19 purchases, version three got 11.

What isn't shown here

The original creatives for each version aren't on this page yet, so I don't claim which change caused the lift.

03

Dental clinic funnel — a demo

Fictional clinic · landing page + Meta ads strategy+

What it is

A worked example for a made-up clinic, Brightside Family Dental — not a client and not a real result. It shows how I'd sell a new-patient visit.

The landing page

A direct-response page: a named $79 offer with a value stack, a guarantee, a comfort promise for nervous patients, every common objection answered, a deadline, and a free price list for visitors who aren't ready to book.

The ad strategy

Audiences, five booking hooks plus a lead-magnet ad, a three-campaign structure, a 30-day test plan, tracking that separates bookings from email sign-ups, and funnel maths with every figure marked as an estimate.
